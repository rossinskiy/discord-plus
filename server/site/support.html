Support

Discord+ is free and stays free. If it saves you time and you want to chip in, the money pays for the server and the hours that go into the project. Everyone who donates, in any amount, gets the Discord+ Supporter badge on their profile.

Get your Supporter badge

  1. Donate

    Any amount, on Boosty or to one of the wallets.

  2. Message me on Telegram

    Write to @jreinject and send this, with your own details filled in:

    Supporter badge Discord ID: 123456789012345678 Donated: Boosty or which coin Proof: Boosty name or transaction hash

  3. Wait for the badge

    I check the donation by hand and add the badge within 24 hours. People who run Discord+ with MyProfile switched on then see it on your profile.

Your Discord ID is a long number, not your username. In Discord open Settings, then Advanced, switch on Developer Mode, right-click your own name and choose Copy User ID.

Check the address first

The same addresses are listed in the project repository, in server/donate.json. Compare them with the ones above before you send anything, so a tampered page cannot send your money somewhere else.
