What changed

Last updated November 18, 2026

November 18, 2026

  • MessageArchive has a new /archive command. It searches the messages Discord+ saved, including deleted ones and old versions of edited ones.
  • /schedule in ScheduledMessages can repeat a message with the repeat option.
  • /stats in PersonalStats shows a bar chart of the messages you sent in the last 14 days.
  • QuietConsole accepts your own words to hide in the console.
  • A new command, pnpm checkPatches, lists the patches that no longer fit the current Discord build.
  • The README is available in Russian, and the FAQ on the home page has three new answers.

November 17, 2026

  • New plugin QuietConsole hides the "Hold Up!" warning and Discord's routine log lines in the DevTools console. You can add your own words to hide.
  • AutoQuest remembers rewards that Discord refused to hand out and no longer tries them again after every restart.
  • GifPaste, the BotClient sticker rule and NoDevtoolsWarning work again on the newest Discord build.
  • AppLock, PersonalStats and ScheduledMessages are now listed in the plugin catalog.
  • The messages of ScheduledMessages, AppLock, Reminders, PurgeMessages, BulkExport, UserTags and AutoLeaveEmptyVC are translated to Russian.

November 2026, earlier

  • MyProfile is built in and always on. Badges of other people refresh every minute, and Discord+ badges show in the Your badges window.
  • AutoQuest works again after Discord changed how its toasts work.
  • ServerCloner, BotClient and ChannelNotes were added to the catalog.
  • The privacy page was rewritten to match what the client and the site do with data.
  • The whole site and the client are available in Russian.
