Plugins

Seventeen plugins written for Discord+, plus the second account window. Every plugin from Vencord still works in the same settings page. All Discord+ plugins start off until you switch them on, except MyProfile, which is built in and always on.

Fun

  • An idle garden that keeps earning while you chat, fishing with a collection of 30 fish, a brick game that plays itself, a fidget board, knife flipping, running in circles, rooftop parkour, and eight calm scenes such as an aquarium, a fireplace and a rainy window.

    Garden 4.32K coins, 3.3 per second
    Fishing Caught a trout and 2 more
    Bricks Level 5, 12 balls
    Illustration, not a screenshot.
    • Open it from the button in the chat bar or with the /lounge command.
    • Progress is saved in a file on your PC, so it survives a reinstall.
    • It is all just games. Nothing in it is sent anywhere.
    Saves data
    a file on your PC
    Discord+ server
    none
    Risk
    none

  • Counts messages you sent, direct messages you received, time in voice, your busiest weekday and your most active DMs. Type /stats in any channel to see the numbers.

    • Counted on this computer only and never sent anywhere.
    • Server messages are counted as sent, never as received.

Tools

  • For video Quests it reports your progress, for game Quests it tells Discord a game is running, and for activity Quests it keeps the session alive. It paces itself like a person and finishes when the Quest is due, not later.

    Quest Play a game for 15 minutes
    8 of 15 min
    Status running, claims the reward when due
    Illustration, not a screenshot.
    • Only works on Quests you accepted yourself.
    • It does not solve captchas.
    • Other users report temporary Quest suspensions for what Discord calls inauthentic quest completion. Use an account you can afford to lose, or leave it off.
    Saves data
    nothing
    Discord+ server
    none

    high, breaks Discord's rules

    Reminder call back, with a chime
    Illustration, not a screenshot.
    • Reminders survive a restart.
    • The plugin settings list your upcoming reminders and let you cancel them.

  • Right-click a server and pick Clone server. Discord+ creates a new server on your account with the same name, icon, roles, role permissions, categories, channels, channel permissions and emojis, at a pace that stays under Discord's rate limits.

    • Only what you can see is copied. Messages, members, bots, webhooks, stickers and invites are not, and the banner needs Boost level 2.
    • A big server takes a while, about 45 requests a minute. You can cancel from the same menu.
    • It sends many requests from your account. Discord's terms do not allow client mods or automation, so this is at your own risk. Do not use it to copy someone else's server and pass it off as theirs.
    Saves data
    nothing
    Discord+ server

  • Logs this client in as a bot, from the plugin settings or the login screen. Your own account is replaced by the bot until you log out. It is ported from DiscordBotClient and VencordDBCPlugin and is experimental.

    • It does nothing until you enter a bot token. Use only bots you own.
    • The token is checked first, so a typo does not log you out of your account.
    • To come back to your account, log out of the bot and log in normally.
    • The token is only sent to Discord.
    Saves data
    your PC

  • Covers Discord with a lock screen until you type your password. It locks when Discord starts, on a hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+L by default) and, if you set it, after some minutes without a mouse or keyboard.

    • It stops someone sitting at your open screen. It does not protect against someone who can open your files.
    • Only a salted hash of the password is stored, on your PC.
    • Nothing happens until you set a password in the plugin settings.
    Saves data
    your PC

    Scheduled for 21:40, in this channel

    • Waiting messages are stored on your PC.

Appearance

  • Give yourself badges and a display name that only you see, even ones that look like Discord's own. Discord+ users also get a member number: the lower it is, the earlier they joined. Others with Discord+ see it as a badge on the profile.

    yourname
    Discord+ OG #7, since October 22, 2026
    Display name only you see this one
    Illustration, not a screenshot.
    • The Discord+ badges (Dev, Tester, Supporter, Founder, Bug Hunter, Artist) are handed out by the Discord+ team. You cannot give them to yourself.
    • To show badges it asks the Discord+ server about people whose profiles you look at, which sends their user IDs. This is built in and cannot be turned off.
    • Your own user ID is sent to the Discord+ server when Discord starts, to join the member list. This is built in and cannot be turned off.
    • Your own badges and display name never leave your PC.
    Saves data
    your PC
    Discord+ server
    built-in lookups, they send user IDs

Built in

  • Press Ctrl+Shift+2 while a Discord window is focused. The second account runs in its own window with a separate login, and both stay connected, so messages and notifications arrive on both.

    Ctrl+Shift+2 Account 1 and Account 2
    Both stay connected
    Illustration, not a screenshot.
    • There is nothing to switch on, it is part of Discord+.
    • Only one window is visible at a time, and the second one does not appear in Alt+Tab.
    • The shortcut only works while a Discord+ window is focused. It is never a system-wide hotkey.
    • Closing the second window hides it instead of closing it.
    Saves data
    a separate login on your PC
